FASE Vision Lab

A desktop application for visual research and art direction: gather and organize references, explore creative directions and manage visual projects.

Visual research

Bring images, notes and links together in a visual workspace.

Reference organization

Sort references by project and by creative direction.

Creative exploration

Compare directions and keep track of decisions.

Compatible services

Optionally connect compatible external services with your own accounts.

Pinterest

Vision Lab offers an optional, read-only connection to your Pinterest account through Pinterest's official authorization (OAuth). It lets you:

  • view your own boards and Pins while researching references;
  • search within your own Pins.

Vision Lab does not offer search across all of Pinterest. Pins are displayed live from Pinterest: they are not stored, not copied into your projects and not mixed with other sources, and every Pin links back to Pinterest. Vision Lab never publishes or changes anything on your account and never uses Pinterest content to train an AI model. You can revoke access at any time, in Vision Lab and in your Pinterest account settings.

Privacy

Vision Lab has no user account and no server: your projects stay on your computer, with no analytics. The data sent to the services you enable is described in the Privacy Policy.
