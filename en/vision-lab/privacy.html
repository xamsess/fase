Privacy Policy — FASE Vision Lab

Last updated: November 13, 2026. The French version is the reference version.

1. Summary

  • FASE Vision Lab is a desktop application for Windows. It has no user account, no server and no analytics, and sends no usage data to the publisher.
  • Your projects, images, notes and settings are stored on your computer. The publisher has no access to them.
  • When you choose to use an external service (AI provider, image library, Pinterest), Vision Lab sends it the data the feature requires. That service processes it under its own privacy policy.

2. Controller and contact

FASE Vision Lab is a personal, non-commercial project developed by a private individual (see the legal notice). For any question about your data: fasecreativesuite@gmail.com.

3. What stays on your computer

DataPurpose
Projects: images you import, notes, links, creative directions, decisions, history of your requestsTo run your projects
Settings and preferencesTo keep your choices
API keys and access tokens of the services you connect, encrypted by Windows and bound to your Windows accountTo connect to the services you chose

This data stays under your control: you can delete it at any time. Uninstalling does not delete your projects. If other FASE applications are installed, they may exchange references with Vision Lab on your computer, without going through the Internet.

4. Microphone (dictation)

Dictation is active only when you click the microphone button, after Windows' permission. Audio is transcribed on your computer; the temporary audio file is deleted right after. No recording is sent over the Internet.

Images and information from an image library (author, licence, link to the original) are displayed in the application; an image is saved in your project only if you import it, when the provider's licence allows it. AI processing that runs on your computer sends nothing over the Internet.

6. Pinterest

Connecting Pinterest is optional and uses Pinterest's official authorization (OAuth 2.0). Vision Lab requests read-only scopes only: boards:read, pins:read and user_accounts:read.

  • Data read: your account name, your boards, the Pins of your boards and the results of a search within your Pins, at the moment you view them.
  • Data kept: only the access token issued by Pinterest, encrypted on your computer. Pins and their information are neither written to disk nor copied into your projects; thumbnails stay in memory only while the application is open.
  • What Vision Lab does not do: it never publishes, edits or deletes anything on your account; Pinterest data is never sent to the publisher or to any third party, combined with other sources, used to train an artificial intelligence model or used for advertising.
  • Revoking access: “Disconnect” in Vision Lab (deletes the token) and, on Pinterest, in your account settings.

7. Legal bases and retention

Exchanges with an external service rely on your request (performing the feature you use) and, for connecting an account, on your consent, which you can withdraw at any time. Data stored on your computer stays there until you delete it; the Pinterest token is deleted as soon as you disconnect.

8. Your rights

You have the rights of access, rectification, erasure, objection, restriction and portability provided by the GDPR. Your local data is directly under your control; for data processed by an external service, contact that service. Contact: fasecreativesuite@gmail.com. You may also lodge a complaint with the CNIL (www.cnil.fr) or your local data protection authority.

9. This website

This website uses no cookies, no analytics and no external fonts or scripts. It is hosted by GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.), which may keep technical logs, such as IP addresses, to operate the service.

10. Changes

This policy is updated when the application's processing changes; the date at the top shows the latest version.
